Stage 2 · Hiking day 3 · 27 October 2026

Via Çoban Plajı to Limanağzı – and on to Kaş

Translated from German

The third and last hiking day began with breakfast at the Habesa Bungalow Hotel, overlooking the sea.

Then we hiked from Ufakdere via Çoban Plajı to Limanağzı. We set off at 9:19. The coastal path was varied, with rocky sections, a stretch across a pebble beach and views of bays, islands and the sea again and again. After 8.85 kilometres and 265 metres of ascent we reached Limanağzı at around half past one.

To round off our three hiking days we went for a swim in the sea. Lunch was lahmacun at the Hakiliki Beach Club.

After that, the hiking group went their separate ways. From Limanağzı we took the boat back to Kaş, where we spent the following days with our friends Deniz and Lütfü. We stayed two nights at the Hotel Ferah.

We started the evening with Deniz and Lütfü on a roof terrace above the harbour. Opposite lies the Greek island of Meis (Kastellorizo), and the clouds broke open once more over the sea.

Afterwards we sat at Restaurant Demeti over meze and rakı.
