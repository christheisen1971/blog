Stage 2 · Hiking day 3 · 27 October 2026

Via Çoban Plajı to Limanağzı – and on to Kaş

Translated from German

The third and last hiking day: from Ufakdere via Çoban Plajı to Limanağzı. We set off at 9:19. The coastal path was varied, with rocky sections and views of bays and the sea again and again. After 8.85 kilometres and 265 metres of ascent we reached Limanağzı at around half past one.

To round off our three hiking days we went for a swim in the sea. Lunch was lahmacun at the Hakiliki Beach Club.

After that, the hiking group went their separate ways. We drove on to Kaş together with our friends Deniz and Lütfü, with whom we spent the following days. We stayed two nights at the Hotel Ferah. In the evening we sat at Restaurant Demeti over meze and rakı.
