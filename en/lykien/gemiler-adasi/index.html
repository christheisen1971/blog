Boat trip to Gemiler Adası

Translated from German

On 26 September, the day before the guided tour began, a boat trip to Gemiler Adası was on the programme.

The small island lies about nine kilometres south of Fethiye, only some 100 metres off the coast, and is uninhabited. Five Byzantine churches were built here between the 4th and 6th centuries, along with the remains of about 40 buildings and 50 Christian graves.

Archaeologists consider it possible that the original tomb of Saint Nicholas of Myra was here. His body is said to have lain in the church carved into the rock until the 650s, when the inhabitants fled an approaching Arab fleet and went to Myra.

The Turkish name means “boat island”; as early as the Middle Ages seafarers called it St Nicholas Island.
