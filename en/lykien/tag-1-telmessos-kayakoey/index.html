Stage 1 · Hiking day 1 · 27 September 2025

Rock tombs, an abandoned village and the way to Ölüdeniz

Translated from German

The guided tour began on 27 September. Part of the group had only landed at Dalaman that morning and was picked up there; together we set off for the first stage. From this day on we stayed at the Kaizen Gate Hotel in Ölüdeniz.

First we visited Telmessos, today's Fethiye. The Lycian rock tombs date from the 4th century BC; the Lycians carved them straight into the rock face.

Then we went on to Kayaköy. Greek Orthodox Christians once lived in the village; it was abandoned in the 1923 population exchange between Greece and Turkey. Today the ruins of some 500 houses and several churches stand there.

From there we hiked towards Ölüdeniz. At the end the watch showed 6.13 kilometres and 286 metres of ascent.
