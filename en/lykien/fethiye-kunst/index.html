Stage 2 · Fethiye · 29 October 2026

An art-filled finale in Fethiye

Translated from German

It was still raining in Kaş in the morning. We drove to Fethiye with Deniz and Lütfü and checked in at the Marina Boutique Hotel.

From the hotel we walked to the Likya Sanat Yolu gallery. Lütfü, who knows the gallerist Ertuğrul Aydemir, had made the contact. The sixth Likya Sanat Yolu artist residency was taking place there during those days. We looked at contemporary art and got talking with the artists and with Ertuğrul Aydemir.

As part of the residency there was a hands-on session led by a Turkish artist who is a professor in Florence – and so I created a picture of my own.

Afterwards we had baklava right near the gallery. We spent the evening at the Italian restaurant Trattoria di Kukina, and our last night at the Marina Boutique Hotel.
