Stage 2 · Fethiye · 29 October 2026

An art-filled finale in Fethiye

Translated from German

It was still raining in Kaş in the morning. We drove to Fethiye with Deniz and Lütfü and checked in at the Marina Boutique Hotel. From the hotel we looked out over the boatyard and the wide bay of Fethiye. Right next door, a pirate ship for excursions sat in dry dock.

From the hotel we walked to the Likya Sanat Yolu gallery. It is run by Ertuğrul Aydemir, a friend of Lütfü's, and his wife. The 6th Likya Sanat Yolu Fethiye Workshop was taking place there, from 25 October to 1 November 2026: for a week, artists work together in the studio above the exhibition hall.

We looked at contemporary art, browsed a wall full of records and got talking with the artists and with Ertuğrul Aydemir.

Upstairs in the studio, work was in full swing: easels, paint, stacks of art magazines and canvases everywhere.

As part of the workshop there was a hands-on session led by a Turkish artist who is a professor in Florence – and so I created a picture of my own.

In the afternoon we had baklava right next door at Ulaşlıoğlu Baklava. We spent the evening at the Italian restaurant Trattoria di Kukina, and our last night at the Marina Boutique Hotel.
