Stage 2 · Looking back · 30 October 2026

Going home and looking back

Translated from German

On the last morning, the bay of Fethiye lay mirror-smooth in the morning haze. Before breakfast Lütfü and I went for a run: 6.24 kilometres in just under 42 minutes along the water.

After breakfast the four of us went down to the marina once more and then shopping: lokum (Turkish delight) to take home, in every colour and flavour.

Deniz and Lütfü then drove us to Dalaman airport. This time the flight home to Düsseldorf went smoothly.

Looking back

This second stage was not a continuous long-distance hike but a mix: three hiking days, with boat trips and swims in quiet bays in between, ancient sites such as Patara, Aperlai and Simena, good food, days with friends and, to finish, the art in Fethiye. The chaotic start and the rain that spoiled our boat trip in Kaş are just as much part of it.

Where we stayed

  • Uygar Hotel, Fethiye (24 October)
  • Flower Hotel, Patara (25 October)
  • Habesa Bungalow Hotel, near Kaş (26 October)
  • Hotel Ferah, Kaş (27 to 29 October)
  • Marina Boutique Hotel, Fethiye (29 October)

Good to know

  • The private Tesla transfer from Dalaman airport to Fethiye cost 125 euros and took about 90 minutes.
  • At the Uygar Hotel we paid 2,000 TRY per person; at the Hotel Ferah, 16,000 TRY for two nights for the two of us.
