The granaries of Bezirgan and ancient Patara

Translated from German

Our first hiking day took us through the area between Sarıbelen and Gökçeören. At the end, the watch showed 9.54 kilometres and almost 300 metres of ascent – including all breaks, we were out for nearly five hours.

In Bezirgan we saw the old wooden granaries that are so typical of the area. They say a lot about how people here in the highlands lived and worked.

Then we went on to Patara. The ancient city was an important centre of the Lycian League, the alliance of the Lycian cities. We visited the monuments and the ancient assembly building and walked down to the beach. Along the way, the group's conversations turned to the political system of the Lycians, the Greek and Roman history of the region, and Nicholas of Myra, who according to tradition was born in Patara.

We spent the night at the Flower Hotel in Patara (Gelemiş).
