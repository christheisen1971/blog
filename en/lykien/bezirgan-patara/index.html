Stage 2 · Hiking day 1 · 25 October 2026

The granaries of Bezirgan and ancient Patara

Translated from German

For us it was the first hiking day, for the group already the second. We left Fethiye early in the morning; as the sun rose, mist still lay over the fields.

Before setting off we had a wide view down to the coast and the bay of Kalkan.

We hiked in the area between Sarıbelen and Gökçeören. At the end, the watch showed 9.54 kilometres and almost 300 metres of ascent – including all breaks, we were out for nearly five hours.

In Bezirgan we saw the old wooden granaries that are so typical of the area. They stand in long rows along the path and say a lot about how people here in the highlands lived and worked.

Then we crossed the plateau, past huge karst rocks and with the sea on the horizon again and again.

In the afternoon we reached Patara. The ancient city was an important centre of the Lycian League, the alliance of the Lycian cities. Right at the start stand the three-arched city gate, an ancient sarcophagus and a large palm grove.

We visited the theatre and the ancient assembly building. Along the way, the group's conversations turned to the political system of the Lycians, the Greek and Roman history of the region, and Nicholas of Myra, who according to tradition was born in Patara.

In the evening light we walked along the ancient colonnaded street, part of which is under water. Greek inscriptions can still be read on many of the stone blocks.

At the edge of the excavations we had one more encounter, with an animal this time.

From the excavation site you look across reeds and water all the way to the sea. Then we went down to the long sandy beach of Patara.

To end the day we sat with the group in the dunes on Patara beach.

We spent the night at the Flower Hotel in Patara (Gelemiş).
