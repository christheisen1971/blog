The day we missed

Translated from German

24 October was the first day of our guided hiking tour. The group set off without us, because the cancelled flight meant we were still on our way to Turkey. The photos from this day were therefore taken by our fellow hikers.

On 24 October we finally landed in Dalaman. A private transfer in a Tesla took us from the airport to Fethiye in about 90 minutes – a very pleasant ride and a welcome contrast to the chaos of getting there. The transfer cost 125 euros.

In Fethiye we checked in at the Uygar Hotel. The next morning we were to join the group.
