Stage 2 · Hiking day 2 · 26 October 2026

From Apollonia down to Aperlai – and by boat through Kekova

Translated from German

In the morning, on the way to the starting point, we stopped above Kaputaş Plajı, the well-known beach between the cliffs on the coast road between Kaş and Kalkan.

On our second hiking day we set off from Kılıçlı shortly before ten, as every morning with a group photo. The path led through the area of ancient Apollonia and then down to the coast at Aperlai: hardly any ascent, but around 370 metres of descent over 7 kilometres. Shortly after one we reached the sea.

Just before the end, the view opened onto the bay of Aperlai. Lycian sarcophagi stand along the path, and one of them now even stands in the water.

Aperlai is a small ancient settlement right on the coast. It was known for producing purple dye from sea snails, and part of the old waterfront now lies under water. Down by the bay, pomegranates and lemons hung from the trees.

Then we boarded the boat. We had to take off our muddy hiking boots, which stayed together on deck, and made ourselves comfortable on the loungers. In the bay of Kocakarı Koyu we jumped into the turquoise water. Meanwhile, lunch was sizzling on the grill at the stern: fresh levrek (sea bass).

The boat took us on through the islands of Kekova, past the bay of Tersane Koyu and the sunken remains of ancient harbours and settlements. Goats were climbing among the old walls. Turquoise water with traces of antiquity in it – one of the most impressive moments of this trip.

In Kaleköy, ancient Simena, we climbed up to the castle. From the top you look out over the little harbour, the islands and the whole bay.

Back in the village we took a break at a small café overlooking the bay.

On the way back, a famous film scene was re-enacted at the bow.

We spent the night at the Habesa Bungalow Hotel near Kaş. For dinner I had köfte, Yeşim had patlıcan, an aubergine dish. Another guest at the table was interested too.
