From Apollonia down to Aperlai – and by boat through Kekova

Translated from German

On our second hiking day we set off from Kılıçlı shortly before ten. The path led through the area of ancient Apollonia and then down to the coast at Aperlai: hardly any ascent, but around 370 metres of descent over 7 kilometres. Shortly after one we reached the sea.

Aperlai is a small ancient settlement right on the coast. It was known for producing purple dye from sea snails, and part of the old waterfront now lies under water.

We swam in the bay of Kocakarı Koyu, and lunch was freshly grilled fish on board a boat. The boat then took us on through the islands of Kekova, past the bay of Tersane Koyu and the sunken remains of ancient harbours and settlements, towards Kaleköy, ancient Simena. Turquoise water with traces of antiquity in it – one of the most impressive moments of this trip.

We spent the night at the Habesa Bungalow Hotel near Kaş. For dinner I had köfte, Yeşim had patlıcan, an aubergine dish.
