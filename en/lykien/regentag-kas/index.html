Stage 2 · Kaş · 28 October 2026

Rain instead of a boat trip

Translated from German

For this day we had planned a private boat trip with Deniz and Lütfü on the "Mevlüt 6". It didn't happen: it rained, at times very heavily, and the trip was cancelled.

So we spent the morning at the Derya Beach Club in Kaş and had lunch there too. Yeşim and Deniz didn't let the rain stop them and went swimming anyway.

For me it was a day of obstacles as well: my MacBook Air had to be reinstalled, and I couldn't find a Wi-Fi connection good enough to download the software.

In the afternoon we strolled through the lanes of Kaş. The real rulers of the town are the cats: they sit in front of shop doors and even sleep in the middle of the jewellery display. In the heart of the old town stands the so-called King's Tomb (Kral Mezarı), a Lycian sarcophagus beneath an old plane tree.

In the evening we went back to Demeti once more – again for meze and rakı, plus grilled octopus and prawns from a sizzling pan. Here too, the cats were never far away.
