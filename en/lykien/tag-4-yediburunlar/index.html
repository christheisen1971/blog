Stage 1 · Hiking day 4 · 30 September 2025

From the old tree to the Yediburunlar lighthouse

Translated from German

The last hiking day began with lovely weather.

Before the start we visited an old tree at a high point of our trip; the watch showed about 1,060 metres. We took a group photo, and a few minutes later I made friends with a goat.

Then we set off. The last stage led south from the area near Alınca to just short of the Yediburunlar lighthouse (“Seven Capes”).

At the end the watch showed 6.09 kilometres and 336 metres of ascent.

After the last day's stage the bus dropped us at the Dalaman Airport Alibaba Hotel, where we spent the last night. The last photo of the tour shows the group in front of the bus.
