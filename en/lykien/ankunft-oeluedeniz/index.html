Stage 1 · Arrival · 24 September 2025

Arriving in Ölüdeniz – and a twisted ankle while jogging

Translated from German

Our first stage on the Lycian Way began on 24 September 2025 with the flight from Düsseldorf to Dalaman. Shortly after landing I took a selfie in the terminal in front of the airport sign.

Deniz and Lütfü picked us up at the airport. We checked in at the Green Pine Hotel in Ölüdeniz, where we stayed for the first three nights.

On the morning of 25 September Lütfü and I went for a run: 2.59 kilometres along the beach at Ölüdeniz, in about 20 minutes at 21 degrees. Unfortunately I twisted my ankle.

Distance 2.59 km · Run time 0:20 h · Avg. pace 7:46 min/km · Avg. heart rate 116 bpm

Measured with an Apple Watch

Afterwards I lay on the beach with my foot up.
