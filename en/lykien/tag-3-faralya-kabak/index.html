Stage 1 · Hiking day 3 · 29 September 2025

Rain above the Butterfly Valley – and a 30th wedding anniversary

Translated from German

On the third day we started in Faralya, under an overcast sky and on a wet road.

Soon the view opened up over the Butterfly Valley (Kelebekler Vadisi): a narrow bay with a sandy beach between steep, pale rock walls.

We continued towards Kabak.

Later it started to rain, and we put on our rain gear.

At the end the watch showed 10.88 kilometres and 373 metres of ascent.

The tour's WhatsApp group also provided a small piece of lore for this day: the carob tree (keçiboynuzu). Its seeds are said to weigh almost exactly the same, around 0.2 grams, which is why they were once used as a unit of weight. The word “carat” goes back to them as well: it comes from the Greek keration, the carob seed.

In the evening we celebrated our 30th wedding anniversary with our travel group.
