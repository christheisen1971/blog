DEENTR

March 17, 2011

Route and itinerary

Translated from German

The route is clearly easier than the "Little Khumbu Circuit" that I walked with Ralf in Nepal, because it goes almost exclusively uphill. That way we can manage the ascent in just a few days. The itinerary is as follows:

Day 1 (24 July)
Morning flight from Istanbul to Van.
Then transfer to Doğubeyazıt at an altitude of about 2,000 m.
Preparations there and some shopping.
Sunset at the Ishak Pasha Palace.
Dinner and overnight stay at the Simer Hotel in Doğubeyazıt.

Day 2 (25 July)
Breakfast at the hotel.
Drive to the village of Eli (2,800 m). The heavy luggage is loaded onto the horses.
From there the roughly five-hour climb to Green Camp (tent camp 1) at 3,200 m begins.
Lunch along the way as a packed lunch.
Arrival at Green Camp in the afternoon.
Night in the tent.

Day 3 (26 July)
This day is for acclimatisation.
After breakfast, the roughly four-hour climb to tent camp 2 at 4,200 m.
Packed lunch there while enjoying the view.
Then back down to Green Camp.
Dinner and night in tent camp 1.

Day 4 (27 July)
After breakfast, climb back up to tent camp 2.
Early dinner and early to bed there, because the summit climb begins before dawn.

Day 5 (28 July)
At about 3 a.m., after breakfast, the roughly six-hour climb to the summit begins.
At about 4,900 m you reach the glacier and have to put on crampons. Depending on the weather, it may also be necessary to go roped up.
Finally, at 5,137 m, you reach the summit of Ararat. In good weather and visibility you can see as far as Iran and Armenia. Eastern Anatolia lies at your feet.
The descent first goes to tent camp 2 at 4,200 m (2 to 3 hours). Lunch there. Then (another 2–3 hours) on to Green Camp.
Dinner and night at Green Camp.

Day 6 (29 July)
After breakfast, on to the base camp.
From there, a drive of about two hours to the hotel.
Relaxing and winding down the trip.
Dinner and night at the hotel

Day 7 (30 July)
Breakfast and check-out at the hotel.
Transfer to the airport.
Return flight to Istanbul.
