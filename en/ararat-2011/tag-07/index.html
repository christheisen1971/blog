Day 7 · July 30, 2011

Flight home

Translated from German

For the third night in a row we could only sleep a little, because we wanted to leave early. Harald and Hülya's Turkish Airlines flight was the first, at 10 a.m. Since we still had at least a two-hour bus ride from Doğubeyazıt to Van ahead of us, we had to get up at 6 a.m. At 6:30 there was a rudimentary breakfast, and by 7 we were already sitting on the bus. We arrived at the airport punctually at 9 and said goodbye to Harald. I was really very glad that he had come along. I had asked him quite spontaneously whether he'd like to come, and he said yes immediately. That's what I call an adventurer. Of course we have already arranged to do the next tour together. Maybe to Ahmet's country of origin: Georgia.

Unfortunately Ahmet and I – our flight wasn't until 12:35 – couldn't check in yet, so we got into a taxi with our luggage and went to a café in town once more. There we drank tea and ate a few pastries.

Finally we too returned to the airport, checked in and flew on time to Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen. It was an unspectacular flight. In Istanbul we were picked up by Yeşim and took Ahmet home. Then it was back to our place, and for me too the adventure was over.
