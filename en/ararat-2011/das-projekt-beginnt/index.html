The project begins

Translated from German

After climbing up to Mount Everest Base Camp #1 with my friend Ralf in October 2009 (see blog), I had actually sworn never to put myself through such an ordeal again. But right at the start of that trip, our fellow travellers predicted that once you start mountaineering, you get hooked. And they were right.
This time it is to be a mountain in Turkey, so that getting there is not so far: Mount Ararat or – as it is called in Turkish – Büyük Ağrı Dağı.
At 5,137 metres, Ararat is almost as high as the area around Base Camp in Nepal. After Kala Patthar (5,550 m), the Cho La Pass (5,370 m) and Mount Everest Base Camp (5,360 m), it will be the fourth-highest point I have reached.
I will probably be accompanied by my two friends Ali and Ahmet.
