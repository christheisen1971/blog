July 20, 2011

Hari is coming

Translated from German

Today an e-mail arrived from Harald in Austria. He lands in Istanbul on Friday. I will pick him up at the airport.
Funny, isn't it: I met Harald on our 2009 trip to Nepal (see photo) and recently asked him quite spontaneously whether he'd like to come along to Ararat. And since he's totally (sports-)crazy, of course he said yes. Anyway, I'm really looking forward to it. Because he's not only a great guy, but with his experience he's an asset to the team. In Nepal he taped my foot very professionally, and I don't know whether I would otherwise have made it to the top.
