The dispute over the coat of arms

Translated from German

Many years ago, when Armenia was still under Soviet control, the Turkish foreign minister of the time angrily complained that Ararat was depicted at the centre of the Armenian coat of arms. In a conversation with his Soviet counterpart Gromyko, he suggested that Armenia was thereby indirectly laying claim to a mountain that was, after all, on Turkish territory. Gromyko replied diplomatically: "If that were so, then Turkey would surely not be allowed to carry the crescent moon on its flag either." (Source: The Sunday Times)
