Presents

Translated from German

This time I had asked for something about Ararat for my birthday. However, there isn't much literature about Ararat.

My friend Ralf gave me the book "Ararat: In Search of the Mythical Mountain" by Frank Westerman. A short review can be found here. I will report on it once I have read it.
From my brother Andreas and my sister-in-law Ilona I received the audiobook of the historical novel "The Forty Days of Musa Dagh" by Franz Werfel. It is about the Armenian resistance at the beginning of the 20th century (see also the Wikipedia entry on the book).
