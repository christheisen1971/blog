Terror in Diyarbakır

Translated from German

What shouldn't have happened has, of course, happened:
Shortly before the trip, and after a really long quiet period, there is another terrorist attack in Diyarbakır in eastern Turkey. And it is no minor attack either: 13 soldiers and 6 terrorists are killed.

To be honest, I considered cancelling the trip. But since the government is doing everything it can to keep the issue from escalating and the tour operator sees no problems, we will probably go ahead with the climb after all. Of course, it may still turn out that we have to cancel the whole thing at the last second. :-(
