July 15, 2011

Ali has a problem

Translated from German

Today Ali, who organised the whole tour, called me and told me that unfortunately he can't take part in the tour.
The background: Ali works for Arab Turkish Bank A&T. Libya is apparently a shareholder, and its funds have been frozen. In Turkey too. So at the beginning of July the Turkish government put the bank under strict financial supervision, replaced the board and has now also cancelled all leave for senior staff for the time being.
Unfortunately that includes Ali. So he and his wife Yaprak won't be coming along. Damn! :-(
