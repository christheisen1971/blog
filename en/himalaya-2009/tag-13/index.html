DEENTR

Day 13 · September 30, 2009

Everest Base Camp (5,360 m) and Gorak Shep (5,130 m)

Translated from German

Today we headed for the big goal of the trip: Mount Everest. First, though, we had to break camp in Lobuche and hike up to Gorak Shep at 5,130 m. It was only a stretch of about 3 hours, but it felt like an eternity. In total it was only 230 metres of ascent, but constantly up and down. And we crossed a glacier area, so there were lots of boulders again.

When we finally arrived we ate a little and then set off together with Jakob, a lone trekker from Bielefeld, for the base camp at the foot of Mount Everest. The walk took about 2 hours and led us past many interesting mountains. The path mainly ran along a glacier, i.e. we walked along the crest of the lateral moraine.

Then the path suddenly turned into the middle of the glacier, because that's the official site of the base camp. But there wasn't much to see. Sure, the glacier and the mountains are great. But Mount Everest was – as unfortunately so often – wrapped in clouds. It was already afternoon, after all. By the way, further up the mountain there's currently the base camp of an Indian military expedition that wants to reach the summit of Mount Everest in the coming days.

So we took a few photos, marvelled at the magnificent surroundings and then went back. By the way, a glacier like this looks like a long rubble heap. If you look more closely, you can see that there's thick ice just below the surface of the rubble.

Whereas it had still been warm on the way there, it cooled down very quickly on the way back.

Back at the lodge we sat together and played cards. After dinner we quickly retired to our rooms, because we were all tired and Kala Patthar is to be climbed at 6 tomorrow morning.
