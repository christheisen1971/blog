I'm off then …

Translated from German

The time has finally come. We're off. To celebrate the day I shaved this morning. But I don't intend to do it again over the next few weeks – for practical and hygienic reasons.

Boarding is in about half an hour. I'm flying with Emirates to Dubai first. There I'll meet Ralf tonight, and tomorrow at 8 we fly on via Bahrain to Kathmandu.

It's about 26 degrees Celsius in Istanbul right now, 35 in Dubai and 29 in Kathmandu. It will only get cooler once we climb higher.

Yesterday I read up a bit more on the health risks. I'm not worried about my fitness, but altitude sickness and the various kinds of diarrhoea do give me a bit of a headache. And I'm not a particularly good drinker, which is exactly what matters up there. Well, it's too late now anyway.

Everything at home is organised. To be honest, I didn't think I'd manage to get everything done right on time. But I did, and now I can go trekking without a worry. A big kiss to my dear Yesim and sweet Dicle. I miss you already :'-(

The next post will come from Dubai. Have a good flight 😀
