Day 12 · September 29, 2009

Lobuche – acclimatisation day

Translated from German

We spent today just at the lodge.

First we had breakfast and then sat in the sun. It's warm as long as the sun shines. As soon as a cloud comes, it turns cool. The lodges here generate electricity with small solar panels or a generator. But the fuel for that has to be carried up from town by yak. Some lodges also have "solar cookers" that use mirrors to focus the heat on one point. A big pot of water stands there and is heated. You can then shower with the water, or it's used for cooking.

Today I showered with this water and washed laundry at the same time. The water was hot, but the shower itself was cold. Especially the concrete floor. I dried the laundry and my hair in the sun.

Then we ate something and lay down for a while.

In the afternoon we played cards. A big group of 23 Canadians has arrived and turned the place of silence into a pretty noisy one. Later there was dinner and we played a few more cards.

That's it for today.

Oh yes: today is our wedding anniversary. Lots of kisses and hugs from here. I love you, Yeşim.
