Day 7 · September 24, 2009

Machermo – 4,400 m

Translated from German

Today we had a short stretch with about 400 metres of ascent. It took us 3 hours without a break. Our destination was the hamlet of Machermo, a cluster of 3–4 lodges. As we had set off at 8:30, we arrived in time for lunch.

The path was quite steep at first, later almost flat. That made walking easier. By the way, I haven't had any sore muscles yet.

Since we're now walking above the tree line, the wind blows very strongly. When the sun shines it's pleasantly warm and you can walk in a T-shirt, but as soon as the sun disappears you need a fleece jacket. On the last climb I even wished I'd had the down jacket. The wind and the clouds literally shoot up the mountain and are ice-cold. Up here everything is foggy. You can see the farmsteads, but the surrounding mountains are in the clouds.

After lunch we climbed around a bit more to acclimatise better. After all, at 4,400 m we're higher than the Grossglockner (3,800 m) and not far from the highest point in the Alps, Mont Blanc (4,808 m).

Tomorrow we'll stay here. We had discussed doing the acclimatisation day in Gokyo, but we'll probably stay here after all.

It's 2 pm now. I'm going to rest a little and shower later. For that, you get a pot of hot water delivered to a draughty little room outside.

I woke up at around 4 pm with a slight headache. Then I showered. It was quite cold, even though the water was warm. It was all over pretty quickly, but the main thing was hair washed and body clean. Luckily I bought one of those quick-drying towels in Namche Bazaar. With my cotton towel it would have been hopeless. It never dries up here anyway. I even think it attracts moisture.

After the shower I joined the others in the lounge. They were already sitting around the stove, which was finally lit. There I could dry my hair and the towel. We chatted and then ordered food. Slowly it got dark and colder outside.

The food was fine. Nothing special. Over dinner we decided to spend the acclimatisation day here in Machermo after all.

After dinner we played 2 rounds of Yahtzee. It was good fun. Poor Harald didn't have good dice today. Oh well, lucky in love then.

Now, at around 9 pm, everyone is going to bed. It's pouring outside. If we're lucky, there'll be no clouds tomorrow. Then we'll get up early and climb about 300 m up a hill with a good view. That's also good acclimatisation training for the upcoming ascent of Gokyo Ri.

Unfortunately we haven't had phone signal for 2 days, and we won't have any tomorrow either. I miss Yeşim and little Dicle very much. Especially when, like now, there's a bit of peace and quiet. Otherwise you have to concentrate quite hard when hiking and are distracted by it. Kisses and hugs to you, my loves.
