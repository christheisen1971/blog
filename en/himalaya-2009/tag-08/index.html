Day 8 · September 25, 2009

Machermo – acclimatisation day

Translated from German

Last night I hardly slept – maybe 2 hours in total. At first I fell asleep but woke up again at 10:30 pm with terrible neck pain. I tried to fall asleep listening to a podcast, but it didn't work. At about 1 am I took a Voltaren 75 and it got better. I was too hot, then too cold again. It's about 8 degrees in the room. I also had a strong, pressing feeling on my chest, but that's nothing unusual for the first night at such an altitude. Breathing is shallower anyway and was repeatedly interrupted by a few quick breaths. I fell asleep at around 4:30 but woke up again after about 45 minutes. Then I dozed and didn't want to go on the excursion.

Still, I went to breakfast at 7:15 and on the hike afterwards. We climbed about 200 m up a hill. You're supposed to have a great view of the mountains from there, but unfortunately it was already too late, as the clouds covered the mountains. Nevertheless, it's important to keep moving to acclimatise. I took a few nice photos. It was very cold and windy. Without a down jacket you can hardly bear it.

The vegetation is very uniform. There are a few evergreen, low shrubs, herbs and small flowers. The rocks seem to be covered by a layer of soil maybe 20–50 cm thick. Again and again a rock pokes out of the ground. On the opposite slopes you can sometimes look straight at the rock, because the soil has slid away.

On the ridge we hiked up today, stones are often piled up or poles with colourful prayer flags put up. The wind makes the flags dance.

Back at the lodge we played some Yahtzee and then ate. Today I chose momos, a kind of small home-made dumplings. Beforehand I always have a soup, usually tomato soup. That helps keep up the high fluid intake.

After eating I lay down, and Ralf was tired too. After about 3 hours we met again in the lounge and played. There isn't much else to do. Interestingly it got very busy today. Yesterday we were the only guests apart from one other couple; today the lodge is almost fully booked. Most come from Australia. We've met quite a few Australians here already.

This afternoon it started raining again – meanwhile we stayed in the dining room, played and had dinner. The rain only stopped a moment ago. Now the sky is clear and starry. But as it's still too early and the moon hasn't fully risen yet, you can only see the outlines of the mountains in the dark. Maybe I'll take a photo later if I wake up again.

We're all glad this boring day is over and that we can carry on hiking in good health tomorrow.
