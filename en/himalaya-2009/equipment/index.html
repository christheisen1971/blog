Equipment

Here is a list of the equipment, which I need (to buy):

  • Backpack (approx. 30 l)
  • Mountain/trekking boots
  • Sandals/sneakers
  • Sleeping bag
  • Jacket
  • Rain jacket (Gore-Tex or similar)
  • Hiking socks/stockings
  • Hiking trousers, shorts
  • Overtrousers (Gore-Tex or similar)
  • Microspikes
  • Gaiters
  • Fleece sweater
  • Fleece jacket
  • Gloves
  • Overmitts
  • Hat / headband
  • Underwear
  • Long johns
  • Shirts
  • T-shirts (technical fabric)
  • Towel
  • Pillowcase
  • Soap, shampoo (biodegradable)
  • Toothbrush, toothpaste
  • Razor
  • Sunglasses
  • Sunscreen SPF 20+
  • Lip balm
  • Water purification
  • Water bottle
  • Thermos flask
  • Head torch or flashlight
  • Camera
  • Spare battery
  • Memory cards or film
  • Money, bank card
  • Passport
  • 3 passport photos
  • Earplugs or cotton wool
  • Sewing kit
  • First-aid kit
  • Matches
  • Watch
  • Pocket knife
  • Reading material
  • Hiking map
  • Maybe: glucose, chocolate, cereal bars, vitamin tablets, etc.
  • (Binoculars)
  • (Telescopic poles)
  • (Altimeter)

First-aid kit contents

  • Plasters, tape
  • Medicine for diarrhoea
  • Insect repellent, e.g. Autan
  • Broad-spectrum antibiotic
  • Headache tablets, e.g. Aspirin
  • Painkillers
  • Nasal drops, e.g. Endrine
  • Cough syrup
  • Personal medication

On top of this I will take my Nikon D60 with the new wide angle lens, the tripod and a solar cell for the iPhone.
