Good night, Dubai

Translated from German

The first half day of the trip is over. I'm spending the night at Ralf's flat on Palm Jumeirah.

He also brought me a few things from Germany: a down jacket, gloves, a mosquito net and a lot of pills. Vitamins, minerals and so on.

It's now half past midnight, and the alarm goes off again at 5.

Good night, Dubai – I really had missed you.
